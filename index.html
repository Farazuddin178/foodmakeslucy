About

“I'm Lucy and I make food.” That's her whole channel description, and it undersells it. Lucy is cooking a national dish for every World Cup country, rebuilding favourites from scratch — Cheez-Its, soda, cultured butter, yogurt that never runs out — and letting the occasional bit of kitchen chaos with her husband roll.

She's a data analyst by training, so this notebook does what she would: it sketches the method behind the shorts, and keeps an eye on the numbers.
